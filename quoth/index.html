Quoth

Hold a key, talk, and tidy text appears wherever you're typing.

Quoth turns your speech into clean text in any app: email, chat, notes. It removes the “um”s, applies your corrections (“two, actually three” becomes 3) and adds the punctuation. Everything runs on your own Mac, so nothing you say goes online. It's free, and anyone can read its code.

Install

Ready in about ten minutes

No Terminal, no technical knowledge. The step-by-step guide walks you through every screen.

  1. Download

    Quoth.dmg (about 15 MB). Open it and drag Quoth into Applications.

  2. Allow it once

    macOS blocks Quoth the first time. Click Done, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

  3. Get the models

    Quoth picks the right size for your Mac (3 to 10 GB) and downloads it in the background.

  4. Three permissions

    Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Then hold Fn and talk.

Why the extra step?

Apple charges developers €99 every year before macOS opens their apps without asking. Quoth is free and I don't earn anything from it, so I publish it without paying that fee. The only cost is one extra click for you, the first time.

Is it safe?

  • Open source: anyone can read all of its code on GitHub.
  • Signed: every release and every update carries the same signature.
  • Private: it works offline and sends nothing anywhere, unless you choose a cloud service yourself.
