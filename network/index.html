How it works

From an export to an introduction

01

Export

LinkedIn lets you download your own connections and messages. WhatsApp adds how often we talk, never what we say.

02

Sort

Everyone gets a closeness ring, a field and seniority from their title, and a guess at where we met.

03

Ask

Type a role, company or topic for a shortlist: a couple of close friends first, then the best of the wider circle.

04

Introduce

Each match comes with a suggested ask. Log the intro and keep track of how it went.

Privacy

Why there are no names here

My contacts didn't sign up for a website. Their names, employers and how close we are stay with me.

It runs on my own Mac. The data never goes to a server or an AI service.

Counts, not content. It sees how often I message someone, never what we said.

This demo is invented. Every person is generated in your browser, and nothing you click leaves this page.

Weak ties carry news

Job news often comes from acquaintances, not close friends. Granovetter, 1973.

Moderately weak ties move careers

A large LinkedIn experiment found they helped job moves most. Rajkumar et al., 2022.

Dormant ties are worth a call

Old contacts reconnect quickly and bring fresh ideas. Levin, Walter & Murnighan, 2011.
